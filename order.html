Delivery FAQs

How long does delivery take?

Typically 30-45 minutes depending on your location and the delivery platform. Pickup orders are ready in 20-30 minutes.

Do you offer contactless delivery?

Yes! Both DoorDash and Uber Eats offer contactless delivery options. Just select this preference when ordering.

Can I schedule an order in advance?

Absolutely! Both platforms allow you to schedule orders up to 7 days in advance—perfect for planning ahead.

What if my food arrives cold or incorrect?

Contact us immediately at (212) 555-8424 or the delivery platform. We'll make it right—satisfaction guaranteed!

Do you include utensils and napkins?

Yes, unless you opt out for sustainability. We include eco-friendly utensils, napkins, and extra sauces with every order.

Can I use restaurant promotions on delivery?

Promotions vary by platform. Follow us on social media for exclusive delivery codes! Direct pickup orders always get 10% off.
