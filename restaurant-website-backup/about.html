Our Story

A Journey of Passion, Culture, and Flavor

How It All Began

Island Spice & Basil was born from an unlikely friendship and a shared dream. In 2018, Chef Marcus Thompson, a third-generation Caribbean cook from Kingston, Jamaica, met Chef Siriporn "Som" Watanabe at a culinary arts competition in Miami. Marcus had grown up watching his grandmother transform simple ingredients into soul-warming Caribbean classics, while Som had trained in the royal kitchas of Bangkok before moving to the United States to explore new culinary horizons.

During a late-night conversation after the competition, they discovered something magical: their culinary traditions, though from opposite sides of the world, shared remarkable similarities—both cuisines celebrated bold flavors, aromatic herbs, fiery chilies, and the harmonious balance of sweet, sour, salty, and spicy. But more importantly, both cuisines were rooted in community, family, and the joy of sharing food with loved ones.

What started as experimental pop-up dinners in Marcus's Brooklyn apartment quickly became the talk of New York's food scene. Their fearless fusion—jerk-spiced curries, tom yum with scotch bonnet heat, Thai basil meeting Caribbean thyme—created something entirely new yet deeply respectful of both traditions. The waiting list grew from 15 people to 150, and they knew they had something special.

In 2020, despite the challenges of opening during a global pandemic, Marcus and Som took the leap and opened Island Spice & Basil in Manhattan's vibrant Tribeca neighborhood. Their mission was simple: to create a space where cultures could connect through food, where unexpected flavors could tell stories of friendship and innovation, and where every guest would leave with not just a full stomach, but a broader perspective on what's possible when we embrace diversity.

Our Continued Passion

Five years later, that same fire that ignited our journey burns brighter than ever. We're not just running a restaurant—we're building a legacy, creating a movement, and proving that the best innovations come from honoring tradition while daring to be different.

🌍

Cultural Bridge

Every dish we serve is a conversation between two rich culinary traditions. We continue to research, travel, and learn—Marcus returns to Jamaica annually to source authentic spices and reconnect with family recipes, while Som visits Thailand to train with master chefs and bring back new techniques.

👥

Community First

Our staff is our family, representing over 15 different countries. We invest in their growth through culinary scholarships, fair wages, and profit-sharing. When our team thrives, their passion translates directly to your plate.

🌱

Sustainable Sourcing

We partner with local farms for produce, source sustainable seafood, and import spices directly from small-batch producers in Jamaica and Thailand. Quality and ethics are never compromised—if we can't source it right, we don't serve it.

💡

Never Stop Innovating

Our menu evolves with the seasons and our creativity. We host monthly "Fusion Lab" nights where we experiment with new combinations, inviting loyal customers to be part of the creative process. Some experiments fail hilariously—others become our next signature dish.
