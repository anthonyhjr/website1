Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reservation?

We highly recommend reservations, especially for Friday and Saturday evenings. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Please inform us of any allergies when making your reservation.

Is there a dress code?

Casual elegant. We want you to feel comfortable while enjoying a special dining experience.

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

Yes! Order through our website or via DoorDash and Uber Eats for delivery throughout NYC.

Can you host private events?

Yes! We have a private dining room for up to 40 guests. Contact us at events@islandspiceandbasil.com for details.

Do you have a bar/happy hour?

Yes! Our bar serves creative cocktails and has happy hour Monday-Thursday, 5-7 PM with $2 off all drinks and appetizers.
