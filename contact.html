Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my services and process

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple website typically takes 2-4 weeks, while complex web applications can take 6-12 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What is your pricing structure?

I offer both fixed-price projects and hourly rates. Simple websites start at $600, while complex applications start at $1,500+. All projects include a free consultation to determine the best pricing model for your needs.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes! I offer maintenance and support packages starting at $100/month. This includes regular updates, security monitoring, bug fixes, and technical support. All projects also include 30 days of free post-launch support.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely! Website redesigns are one of my specialties. I can modernize your design, improve functionality, optimize performance, and migrate your existing content while maintaining SEO rankings.

What technologies do you work with?

I work with modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Vue.js, Node.js, and various databases. I stay updated with the latest frameworks and tools to deliver cutting-edge solutions.

How many revisions are included?

Most projects include 2-3 rounds of revisions during the design phase and 1-2 rounds during development. Additional revisions can be accommodated and will be discussed upfront in the project scope.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes! I work with clients from all over the world. All communication is done via email, video calls, and project management tools. I'm experienced in remote collaboration and ensure smooth communication throughout the project.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely! All websites I build are fully responsive and mobile-first. They'll look great and function perfectly on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. Mobile optimization is included in every project.
